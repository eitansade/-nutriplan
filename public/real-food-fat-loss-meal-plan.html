What a realistic fat loss meal plan needs

Protein at most meals
Eggs, Greek yogurt, chicken, turkey, steak, salmon, cottage cheese, and protein-friendly comfort meals.
Carbs that support life
Rice, potatoes, pasta, oats, sourdough, fruit, and meals that help you train and feel human.
Flexible structure
A plan should survive work, cravings, weekends, restaurants, and imperfect days.

Healthy food should still feel like food

NutriPlan focuses on home-style meals that feel normal: garlic butter steak with crispy potatoes, creamy eggs on sourdough, chicken rice bowls, high-protein pasta, Greek yogurt bowls, tacos, burgers, and simple snacks.

Why people restart every Monday

Diets often fail because they are too strict for real life. People restrict too hard, get hungry, lose momentum, feel guilty, and promise to start again next week. A better plan is not the hardest plan. It is the one you can repeat.

Start with structure, not shame

NutriPlan helps you build a practical meal plan around your body, goal, schedule, and real food preferences. Calories and macros are estimates, not medical advice or guarantees. The goal is clarity, consistency, and healthier choices without obsession.
