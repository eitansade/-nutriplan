# NutriPlan Passive Growth System

Goal: turn TikTok attention into trust, email/account creation, plan purchases, and repeat visits without requiring the owner to manually sell every day.

## Core positioning

NutriPlan is not a punishment diet. It is realistic nutrition for people who want to feel lighter, eat real food, build muscle, lose fat, and stop restarting every Monday.

Primary emotional promise:
"Finally, something realistic."

Primary conversion promise:
"Build a plan around your real life."

## TikTok traffic path

1. Hook: emotional pain or relatable food problem
2. Content: short real-life example
3. CTA: "Build your plan — link in bio"
4. Landing page: hero confirms the same promise
5. Trust: no extreme dieting, real food, realistic structure
6. Offer: Pro plan as default
7. Activation: pay, sign up with PayPal email, refresh access

## Weekly organic content rhythm

Monday:
Post "Monday diet cycle" meme or story.

Tuesday:
Post a real food meal idea: chicken bowl, steak potatoes, yogurt bowl, eggs on sourdough.

Wednesday:
Post "you're not lazy, your plan is broken" emotional reel.

Thursday:
Post gym-stuck content: "the last level is food structure."

Friday:
Post weekend survival content: restaurant choices, cravings, realistic meals.

Saturday:
Post recipe close-up or meal prep.

Sunday:
Post planning/reset content: "stop restarting, build a plan."

## Micro trust checklist

Every public asset should include at least one:

- Real food examples
- "Results vary" or no-guarantee honesty
- "Not medical advice" when nutrition/health claims get specific
- No fake before/after transformations
- No extreme promises
- No shame language
- CTA that clearly says what happens next
