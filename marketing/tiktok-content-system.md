# NutriPlan TikTok Content System

Core message:
"You can finally eat like a normal human being and still become healthier."

## Content Pillars

1. Anti-diet relief
- Stop starving yourself.
- Stop restarting every Monday.
- You are not lazy. Your plan is broken.
- Healthy should not feel like punishment.

2. Real food proof
- Steak and potatoes that still fit.
- Eggs on sourdough.
- Chicken bowls with sauce.
- High-protein pasta.
- Yogurt bowls that feel like dessert.

3. Stuck-at-80% fitness audience
- Already training but stuck.
- Eating pretty good but still guessing.
- The last level is consistency.
- You do not need harder workouts. You need food structure.

4. Busy-life structure
- Workday meals.
- Late-night cravings.
- Takeout prevention.
- Sunday plan for the week.
- What to eat when motivation disappears.

5. Founder/brand story
- Modern diet culture is exhausting.
- Food should support your life, not control it.
- Real progress is built through repeatable weeks.

## Hook Bank

- "Stop starving yourself."
- "Still restarting your diet every Monday?"
- "You are not lazy. Your plan is broken."
- "Healthy eating should feel like home."
- "If you train but still feel stuck, watch this."
- "Your food does not need to be perfect. It needs structure."
- "This is for people stuck at 80%."
- "You can eat potatoes and still make progress."
- "Most diets fail on Tuesday, not Monday."
- "Your meal plan should understand real life."

## Weekly Organic Posting Rhythm

Monday: Monday diet cycle / emotional reset.  
Tuesday: real food meal video.  
Wednesday: stuck-at-80% fitness hook.  
Thursday: grocery/simple prep content.  
Friday: weekend eating without chaos.  
Saturday: cozy meal / lifestyle content.  
Sunday: plan-your-week CTA.

## CTA Rotation

- "Start here - link in bio."
- "Build your plan - link in bio."
- "Try NutriPlan today."
- "Real food. Real life. Real progress."
- "Stop guessing. Start planning."

## Caption Formula

Line 1: emotional pain or identity.  
Line 2: simple reframing.  
Line 3: what NutriPlan helps with.  
Line 4: CTA + website.  
Line 5: hashtags.

Example:
```text
You are not lazy. Your plan just does not fit real life.

NutriPlan helps you build realistic meals around your goal, schedule, and normal food.

Start here: https://nutriplan-taupe.vercel.app

#weightloss #healthyfood #mealprep #nutrition #wellness
```

## What To Avoid

- Fake body transformations.
- Medical promises.
- "Lose X pounds fast" guarantees.
- Shame language.
- Aggressive gym-bro language.
- Claims that make food feel obsessive.
