# NutriPlan Premium TikTok / Canva Video Concepts

Website CTA for every concept: https://nutriplan-taupe.vercel.app  
Primary CTA language: "Start here - link in bio", "Build your plan - link in bio", "Try NutriPlan - link in bio"

Creative rules:
- Do not use fake transformation photos.
- Do not use fake reviews.
- Do not promise guaranteed fat loss or medical outcomes.
- Keep the tone dark, premium, warm, cinematic, and human.
- Make the first 2 seconds impossible to ignore.
- Show real food, real life, and simple structure.
